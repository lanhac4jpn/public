Why Foreign Visitors Are Amazed by Convenience Store Onigiri Wrapping
コンビニおにぎりの包み方に感動する外国人

A: My friend from overseas was amazed by the way convenience store onigiri is wrapped.
B: I understand why. The package keeps the seaweed separate from the rice until you open it.
A: Yes. He said it was clever because the seaweed stayed crispy.
B: Exactly. The packaging may look simple, but it is designed to keep the onigiri fresh and easy to eat.

Question:
Why was his friend amazed by the onigiri packaging?

Choices:
A. Because it keeps the seaweed separate and crispy.
B. Because the package is very large and colorful.
C. Because it makes the rice taste sweet.
D. Because people can cook the onigiri inside the package.
