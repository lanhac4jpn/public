What Is a Typical Japanese Breakfast Like?
日本の朝ごはんってどんな感じ？

A: What do people usually eat for breakfast in Japan?
B: A traditional Japanese breakfast often includes rice, miso soup, grilled fish, and some vegetables.
A: That sounds quite different from my usual breakfast of bread and coffee.
B: Yes, but many Japanese people also eat bread or cereal now. Breakfast habits have become more varied.

Question:
What does she say about breakfast in Japan today?

Choices:
A. Everyone eats grilled fish every morning.
B. People never eat bread for breakfast.
C. Many people also eat bread or cereal.
D. Traditional Japanese breakfast does not include rice.
