312 リスニング台本

What Are Traditional Japanese Sweets, Wagashi?
日本の伝統的なお菓子「和菓子」とは？

A: I've seen beautiful Japanese sweets called wagashi. What are they usually made from?
B: Many wagashi are made from ingredients such as sweet bean paste, rice flour, and mochi.
A: They often look like flowers or leaves. Is there a reason for that?
B: Yes. Wagashi often express the beauty of the seasons, so their colors and shapes change throughout the year.

Question:
What does she say about the appearance of wagashi?

Choices:
A. Their colors and shapes often reflect the seasons.
B. They always look the same throughout the year.
C. They are only made in the shape of flowers.
D. Their appearance is not important.
