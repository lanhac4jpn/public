Types of Hot Pot Dishes and the Joys of Winter
鍋料理の種類と冬の楽しみ

A: Why are hot pot dishes so popular in Japan during winter?
B: Because they warm you up, and everyone can cook and eat together around the same pot.
A: What kinds of hot pot dishes do people enjoy?
B: There are many kinds, such as sukiyaki and shabu-shabu. Each one uses different ingredients and has its own flavor.

Question:
Why does she say hot pot dishes are popular in winter?

Choices:
A. Because people usually eat them cold.
B. Because they warm people up and can be enjoyed together.
C. Because they only use vegetables.
D. Because people do not need to cook them.
