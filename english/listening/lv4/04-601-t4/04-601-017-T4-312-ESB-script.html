312 リスニング台本

Pairing Japanese Tea with Japanese Food
日本茶と和食の相性

A: Why is Japanese tea often served with Japanese food?
B: Its light and refreshing taste goes well with many dishes and does not hide their natural flavors.
A: Do people drink the same kind of tea with every meal?
B: Not always. For example, green tea is common, while roasted tea has a warm flavor that also matches many Japanese dishes.

Question:
Why does she say Japanese tea goes well with Japanese food?

Choices:
A. Because it makes every dish taste sweet.
B. Because it has a light taste and does not hide the natural flavors of the food.
C. Because people only drink it with expensive meals.
D. Because it is always served cold.
