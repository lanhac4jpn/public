Conversation Etiquette at a Sushi Restaurant
お寿司屋さんでの会話のマナー

A: Is there anything I should remember when talking with the chef at a sushi restaurant?
B: It is fine to ask questions, but try to speak politely and avoid interrupting the chef when the restaurant is busy.
A: That makes sense. Can I ask the chef which fish is recommended that day?
B: Of course. Showing interest in the food is usually welcome, as long as you respect the chef and other customers.

Choices:
A. Speak politely and avoid interrupting the chef.
B. Talk loudly to get the chef's attention.
C. Avoid asking any questions about the sushi.
D. Speak only when the restaurant is empty.
