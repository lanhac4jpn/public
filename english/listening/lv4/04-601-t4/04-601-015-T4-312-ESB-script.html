The Origin and Customs of Setsubun Ehomaki
節分の「恵方巻き」の由来と風習

A: I've heard that people eat a special sushi roll called ehomaki on Setsubun. How do they eat it?
B: They usually face the lucky direction of the year and eat the whole roll without cutting it.
A: That's interesting. Has this custom always been popular all over Japan?
B: No. It is said to have roots in Osaka, and it later became widely known across Japan.

Question:
What does she say about the custom of eating ehomaki?

Choices:
A. People usually cut it into many small pieces first.
B. People eat it only at restaurants in Osaka.
C. People face the lucky direction and eat the whole roll.
D. People eat it on New Year's Day.
