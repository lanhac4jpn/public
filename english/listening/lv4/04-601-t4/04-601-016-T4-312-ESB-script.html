Okonomiyaki vs. Monjayaki
お好み焼き vs もんじゃ焼き

A: What's the main difference between okonomiyaki and monjayaki?
B: Okonomiyaki is usually thick and pancake-like, while monjayaki has a softer and more liquid texture.
A: I see. Do people cook both of them on a hot iron plate?
B: Yes. People often cook them at the table, but monjayaki is usually eaten directly from the hot plate with a small spatula.

Question:
What does she say about monjayaki?

Choices:
A. It is usually eaten cold.
B. It has a softer and more liquid texture than okonomiyaki.
C. It is always cooked in a large pot.
D. It is exactly the same as okonomiyaki.
