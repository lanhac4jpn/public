一般動詞 現在形→疑問文書き換え 三単現 その２

問題

以下の文を疑問文に書き換えて下さい。

問題 1: Ken gets up at seven every morning. - ケンは毎朝7時に起きます。

解答: Does Ken get up at seven every morning?

問題 2: Mary teaches English at this school. - メアリーはこの学校で英語を教えます。

解答: Does Mary teach English at this school?

問題 3: My father drives to work every day. - 私の父は毎日車で仕事へ行きます。

解答: Does my father drive to work every day?

問題 4: My mother washes the dishes after dinner. - 私の母は夕食後に皿を洗います。

解答: Does my mother wash the dishes after dinner?

問題 5: He studies math after school. - 彼は放課後に数学を勉強します。

解答: Does he study math after school?

問題 6: She carries a bag to school. - 彼女は学校にかばんを持って行きます。

解答: Does she carry a bag to school?

問題 7: Tom does his homework in the evening. - トムは夕方に宿題をします。

解答: Does Tom do his homework in the evening?

問題 8: Jane goes shopping on Sundays. - ジェーンは日曜日に買い物に行きます。

解答: Does Jane go shopping on Sundays?

問題 9: My brother fixes his bike on weekends. - 私の兄は週末に自転車を修理します。

解答: Does my brother fix his bike on weekends?

問題 10: My sister practices the piano every day. - 私の姉は毎日ピアノを練習します。

解答: Does my sister practice the piano every day?
